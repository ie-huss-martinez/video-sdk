Requires `@hussmartinez/ui` 0.14.3 or later. Its announcer says every message sent in the same moment, such as a new warning and "Undone.", rather than only the last one, so the editor now leaves that to `ui`, and its "Undone." and "Redone." announcements end with a full stop, so they read as sentences when joined. In a right-to-left layout, `ui` 0.14.3 also mirrors the icons that point along time, such as the step and move arrows. If your app also depends on `@hussmartinez/ui`, update it to ^0.14.3, so there's one copy.
