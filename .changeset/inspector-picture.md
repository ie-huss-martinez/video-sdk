The inspector has a second group, for the selected clips' picture: "Rotate left" and "Rotate right", a quarter turn each; how the picture fills the frame, "Show all" or "Fill"; and, with one clip selected, its crop in pixels, with a button that removes it. The catalogue gains the messages for it, under `inspector.*`, and `inspector.empty` now reads "Select a clip to change its sound and picture."
