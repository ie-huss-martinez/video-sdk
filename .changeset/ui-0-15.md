Requires `@hussmartinez/ui` 0.15.0 or later, the version the family's editors now share. Its `Slider` cancels a gesture with Escape, and the editor uses that. Pressing Escape while dragging the inspector's volume slider, or while holding an arrow key on it, puts the volume back where the gesture started, commits nothing, and stops playing the drag's preview. If your app also depends on `@hussmartinez/ui`, update it to ^0.15.0, so there's one copy.
